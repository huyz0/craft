# Confluence publication evidence

Date: 10 November 2026. Internal implementation only. No real tenant, credential provisioning, publication, deployment or independent consequential review occurred.

## Implemented

- `crates/craft-provider-confluence/src/pages/`: closed create/update inputs, explicit body representations and placement, observed current-page fingerprints, revision-bound native POST/PUT preparation and operation-specific result interpretation.
- `crates/craft-cli/src/confluence_pages.rs` and `confluence_pages/completion.rs`: trusted exact site/space/grant/actor and independent write allowances; sealed credential access, all required scopes before native reads, original dual-clock observation freshness, dynamic policy checks through the claimed-send callback, durable send/completion evidence and separate credential-cache receipts.
- `crates/craft-provider-confluence/tests/pages.rs`: seven passing tests cover root/homepage/parent placement, wrong space/parent/lifecycle, omitted content preservation, native body shapes, stale version/title/body/identity, strict/null/no-op input, response/refusal/unknown outcomes and independent pinned route/scope inventory comparison.
- `crates/craft-cli/src/confluence_pages/tests.rs`: three passing local tests cover apply/policy/target/output/input refusal, both expired/backward clocks, and uncertain claim receipts.
- `crates/craft-cli/tests/confluence/publication.rs`, included by the existing feature-gated Confluence host target: three passing owned HTTPS tests span seventeen scenarios. Five cover actual root/homepage/parent POST and title/body PUT payloads, recorded completion and replay refusal. Five cover disconnect, rate limit, documented refusal, undocumented success and incomplete success with retained returned identity/cache receipts. Seven cover before-auth/after-identity/after-read/claimed policy refusal, missing scopes, changed published revision and expired original evidence. Refused scenarios observe no native write.
